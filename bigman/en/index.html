About

BIGMAN Group began in 2015, when a lifelong hospitality professional — a bartender since childhood, later a consultant to others — opened the AYVAZ meyhane in Ankara.

Today the group carries five distinct brands. It prefers to work outside the industry’s usual moulds, puts people first, and is preparing to add new and unconventional brands in the years ahead.
