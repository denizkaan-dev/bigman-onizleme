Founder

Gürkan Ayvaz

A journey that began drying tarhana beside his mother, and has not stopped since.

Born in Germany on 28 October 1975. His family returned to Turkey when he was eight, and he has lived there since — primary school in Uşak, secondary school in İzmir and then Uşak, followed by pharmacy at Ankara University and business administration through the open university.

It started at his mother’s side, helping make tarhana and salça as a small child. With the food culture he learned from her, he stepped behind a bar while still barely more than a boy. Bar chef, general manager, and now founder of BIGMAN Group — the journey continues.

He likes work that goes past what has already been done. Over the years he has had a hand in training many of the people now behind the trade’s best projects.
