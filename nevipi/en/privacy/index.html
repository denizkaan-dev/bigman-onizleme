Privacy

Last updated 4 November 2026

This English text is provided for convenience. The Turkish version is the binding one.

This notice explains, under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), how we process your personal data when you shop on the NEVİ pi website.

Data controller

[Company name], [Registered address]. Contact: [Email], KEP: [Registered e-mail (KEP)].

What we process

  • Name, email and mobile number,
  • Delivery and invoice address; for company invoices, company name, tax office and number,
  • Your order: goods, amounts, note, order and payment status,
  • Your IP address at the time of the order, for transaction security.

Your card details never reach us: the payment page belongs to PayTR and only PayTR processes them.

Why

To take, prepare, ship and discuss your order (performance of the contract), to invoice and keep commercial records (legal obligation), and to prevent fraudulent orders (legitimate interest). We do not use your data for marketing and do not sell it.

Who receives it

PayTR for payment; [Carrier] for delivery; our accountant and e-invoice provider; the hosting service that stores the site and orders ([Hosting provider and server country]); public authorities when they lawfully ask.

Cookies and your browser

We use no advertising or tracking cookies. Your basket is kept only in your own browser’s local storage until you order. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which passes your IP address to Google. PayTR’s payment page may use its own cookies for secure payment.

How long

Order and invoice records are kept for the period the law requires (10 years for commercial books and documents), then deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK you may ask whether and how your data is processed, to whom it is transferred, to have it corrected or erased, to object to automated decisions, and for compensation for unlawful processing. Write to [Registered address], to [Registered e-mail (KEP)] by KEP, or to [Email] from the email address you gave us. We answer within 30 days, free of charge.
