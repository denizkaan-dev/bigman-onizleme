Distance sales agreement

Last updated 4 November 2026

This English text is provided for convenience. The Turkish version is the binding one.

Article 1. Parties

Seller: [Company name], MERSİS [MERSİS number], [Tax office and number], address: [Registered address], phone: [Phone], email: [Email], KEP: [Registered e-mail (KEP)].

Buyer: the consumer who places the order, named with contact and delivery details on the order form.

Article 2. Subject

This agreement sets out the rights and obligations of the parties for goods ordered online from the NEVİ pi website, under Turkish Consumer Protection Law No. 6502 and the Distance Contracts Regulation.

Article 3. Goods, price and payment

The goods, quantities, VAT-inclusive prices, delivery fee and total are as shown on the checkout page and in the order confirmation email, which form part of this agreement. Payment is by card on PayTR’s secure page; the order is final only once payment is approved.

Article 4. Delivery

Goods are sent by [Carrier] to the address on the order, within [number of days] working days of approved payment and never later than 30 days. Check the parcel on delivery and have the courier record any damage.

Article 5. Right of withdrawal

For unopened goods the buyer may withdraw within 14 days of delivery without reason or penalty, by writing to the seller’s email or KEP address. The seller refunds everything paid, delivery included, to the same card within 14 days of the notice. The buyer returns the goods within 10 days of the notice. See Returns and withdrawal.

Article 6. Exceptions

There is no right of withdrawal for perishable goods or goods that may pass their best-before date, nor for goods whose protective seal or packaging has been opened and which cannot be returned for health and hygiene reasons.

Article 7. Faulty goods

If goods are faulty, the buyer has the remedies of Article 11 of Law No. 6502: withdrawal, a price reduction, free repair, or replacement with a sound item.

Article 8. Personal data

The buyer’s personal data is processed as described in Privacy.

Article 9. Disputes

Consumer Arbitration Committees, within the yearly monetary limits, and Consumer Courts above them, have jurisdiction.

Article 10. Entry into force

The buyer accepts the Pre-contract information and this agreement electronically at checkout; the agreement is formed on completed payment, emailed to the buyer and kept by the seller.
