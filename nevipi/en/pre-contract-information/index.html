4. Payment

Payment is taken by credit or debit card on the secure page of PayTR, a licensed payment institution. Your card details never reach the seller and are not stored by us.

5. Delivery

Orders are sent to the address you give, by [Carrier], within [number of days] working days of payment, and never later than the legal limit of 30 days.

6. Right of withdrawal

For unopened goods you may withdraw within 14 days of delivery without giving a reason and without penalty, by writing to the email or KEP address above. By law there is no right of withdrawal for perishable goods or goods that may pass their best-before date, nor for goods whose seal or packaging has been opened and which cannot be returned for health and hygiene reasons. See Returns and withdrawal.

7. Complaints

Write to the seller first. Within the monetary limits set each year by the Ministry of Trade you may apply to the Consumer Arbitration Committee where you live or where you bought, and above them to the Consumer Court.

8. The contract

When you accept this information and complete payment, the Distance sales agreement is formed. It is emailed to you with your order and kept by the seller.
