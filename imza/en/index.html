An ocakbaşı thatrefuses the mould.

Opened in November 2024. A cocktail list, prosecco and champagne, a long meze table, its own decor and music — a new idea of the ocakbaşı in Ankara.

MenuReservations0532 157 31 53

İMZA Ocakbaşı opened in Ankara in November 2024 to serve a grill culture that refuses the usual mould: a cocktail list, prosecco and champagne, an unusually long meze table, its own decor and its own music.

In the conversations at its tables — and in the look on a guest’s face at every forkful — it became a new idea of dining out in Ankara.
