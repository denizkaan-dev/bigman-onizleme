About

In Ankara, everyone knows AYVAZ.

AYVAZ opened as a fish meyhane on Tunus Caddesi, Ankara, in January 2015, and has been serving — and setting firsts — ever since.

Alongside its own house meze and dishes, it set out to serve the finest versions of the classics of Anatolia.

With seating for 180, a deep bar list, a cocktail menu and a wide kitchen, it is one of the most closely watched and most copied houses in Ankara.

A second house opens in the Alaçatı – Çeşme area in summer 2027.
